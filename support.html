Support

Need help with StockEdge? Email support@pslmsound.com and include your device model and what you were doing. We usually reply within two business days.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. StockEdge has no accounts. To keep the same inventory on your iPhone and iPad, turn on Settings → iCloud sync on each device (same Apple Account).
  • Does it work offline? Yes. Changes save on the device first and sync when you are back online.
  • Two devices sold the last unit. Why is stock negative? Both sales are kept, so the item shows as oversold. Restock or recount it to fix the number.
  • The studio look didn't cut out my photo. When the garment fills the photo there is no background to remove, so StockEdge keeps the whole shot and balances the light. For a cut-out on a backdrop, photograph the whole garment laid flat or on a hanger with floor or wall around it.
  • How do I move my data? Settings → Data → Export full backup saves everything, including photos, as one file you can restore later.
  • How do I remove my data from iCloud? Settings → iCloud sync → Delete StockEdge data from iCloud. Inventory saved on your devices is kept.
