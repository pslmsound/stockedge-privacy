StockEdge

Privacy Policy · Last updated: 2026-11-20

StockEdge is an inventory app for apparel stores. It has no accounts and no servers of its own: your inventory is saved on your device and, if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own private iCloud. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking, we cannot see your data, and we never sell it.

On your device

  • Your inventory (styles, variants, stock, history, prices and costs), product photos and store branding are stored in the app's private storage on your device, protected by iOS data protection.
  • Photo tools (background removal, the studio look, color and category suggestions) run on your device using Apple's frameworks. Photos are not uploaded to do this.
  • The AI Studio chat runs on your device. Where Apple Intelligence is available it uses Apple's on-device model; otherwise it uses simple built-in commands. Chat messages are not saved or sent anywhere, and stock only changes after you approve it.
  • Exports (JSON backups, CSV files) and shared product cards go only where you choose to save or share them.
  • Camera and photos: the camera is used only when you take a product photo. The photo picker gives the app only the images you select.

iCloud sync (optional, off until you turn it on)

  • When you turn on Settings → iCloud sync, StockEdge keeps a copy of your inventory, stock history, store branding and product photos in the private iCloud database of the Apple Account signed in on your device, so your iPhone and iPad stay in step.
  • This data is stored by Apple under Apple's privacy policy and counts toward your iCloud storage. Item details and stock history are end-to-end encrypted; photos are encrypted by iCloud. We (the developer) cannot access your private iCloud data, and it is never sent to any server of ours.
  • Delete StockEdge data from iCloud (Settings → iCloud sync) removes it from iCloud for all your devices. You can also manage it in the Settings app under your Apple Account → iCloud. Turning sync off keeps the inventory on your device.

Your data

  • You can view, correct and export everything in the app at any time (Settings → Data → Export full backup). Deleting the app removes the data stored on that device.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, analytics, tracking or data brokers.
  • We do not collect, sell or share your personal information, and we do not use your data to train AI models.
  • StockEdge is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.
