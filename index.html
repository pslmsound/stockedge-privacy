StockEdge

Privacy Policy · Last updated: 2026-11-19

StockEdge is an inventory app for apparel stores. You can use it without an account, and then everything stays on your device. If you sign in, your store's inventory and photos are kept in a private cloud store so your iPhone and iPad stay in step. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking, and we never sell your data.

Without an account

  • Your inventory (styles, variants, stock, history, prices and costs), product photos and store branding are stored only in the app's private storage on your device, protected by iOS data protection. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Photo tools (background removal, the studio look, color and category suggestions) run on your device using Apple's frameworks. Photos are not uploaded to do this.
  • The AI Studio chat runs on your device. Where Apple Intelligence is available it uses Apple's on-device model; otherwise it uses simple built-in commands. Chat messages are not saved or sent anywhere, and stock only changes after you approve it.
  • Exports (JSON backups, CSV files) and shared product cards go only where you choose to save or share them.
  • Camera and photos: the camera is used only when you take a product photo. The photo picker gives the app only the images you select.

With an account (Account & sync)

Signing in is optional. You can sign in with Apple or with a sign-in link sent to your email.

  • Account details: an account ID and your email address. With Sign in with Apple, this may be a private relay address from Apple if you chose to hide your email. We do not ask Apple for your name.
  • Your store's data: the store name and branding (name, tagline, logo, colors); your catalog (categories, styles, SKUs, brands, descriptions, sizes, colors, barcodes, costs, prices and reorder settings); stock history (each change's quantity, reason, note, time and the account that made it); product photos and edited versions; and records of photo edits and category or color suggestions.
  • Why: only to keep your store the same on each device you sign in on. Your store's data can be read only by accounts that are members of that store; today, that is only your account.
  • Where: the data is hosted by Supabase on servers in the United States (US East, Ohio). It travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Like any web service, the host receives your IP address and records technical details such as sign-in times to keep the service secure.
  • Sign-in: Sign in with Apple is provided by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Sign-in link emails are sent by Supabase's email service.
  • On your device: your sign-in is stored in the iOS Keychain on that device only. The inventory itself is still saved on the device first, so the app keeps working offline.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Cloud data is kept until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Delete account (Settings → Account & sync) immediately deletes your account, every store you alone own, its stock history and its uploaded photos from our service. Copies in the hosting provider's backups, if any, expire within 30 days. The inventory on the device you are holding is kept; to remove it as well, delete the app.
  • Sign out stops syncing on that device and keeps its inventory.
  • You can view and correct your data in the app at any time, and export all of it (Settings → Data → Export full backup).

What we don't do

  • No advertising, analytics, tracking or data brokers.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information, and we do not use your data to train AI models.
  • StockEdge is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.
